# The plot (draft for you to edit)

Emerald Remix keeps **Emerald's story beats**: every scene, map and event script already exists, so
changing who's in them and what they say is cheap, while new events are expensive. What changes is
*why* it all happens: the three legendary cats, your friends' starters, and two villain teams with
very New York motives. Everything here is a draft; cross out anything and write your own.

## The idea in one paragraph

Long ago, **Haleeb** spread the clouds and **Ceaser** brought the sun, and their endless argument
over the weather nearly wrecked the land until **Snickers** came down from the storm clouds and ended
it with one swipe. Haleeb fell asleep on a cloud bank under the sea (rain falls wherever she naps),
and Ceaser lay down in a cave and hasn't stood up in a century. Now two companies want them awake:
**Team Drizzle**, a delivery startup that makes money every time it rains, and **Team Heatwave**,
an iced-drink chain that wants summer to never end. Professor Mo just wants a cold drink and for his
Ahmed and Omar to stop fighting.

## The villains

| | Team Drizzle (replaces Team Aqua) | Team Heatwave (replaces Team Magma) |
|---|---|---|
| Who | A food-delivery startup: "It's raining? Stay in. We'll bring it." | A chain of iced-drink stands: "Too hot? Grab a Heatwave." |
| Wants | Wake Haleeb so it rains forever and nobody goes outside again | Wake Ceaser so it's summer forever and everyone's always thirsty |
| Leader | **Rainer**, the CEO in a raincoat (replaces Archie) | **Blaze**, the founder in sunglasses (replaces Maxie) |
| Admins | **Puddles** and **Mist** (replace Shelly and Matt) | **Scorch** and **Sizzle** (replace Tabitha and Courtney) |
| Grunts | Delivery riders with insulated bags | Drink-stand staff with paper hats |
| Running joke | Grunts keep apologizing for late deliveries mid-battle | Grunts try to sell you a drink before every battle; Mo always buys one |

Professor Mo is a regular at Heatwave stands, which makes him awkward about fighting them.

## What's already in the game

- The three cats, with their weather abilities (Haleeb's rain, Ceaser's sun, Snickers calming both),
  so beats 13–17 already play out with them; only the dialogue is still Emerald's beyond the names.
- Their signature moves (Milk Shower, Royal Pounce, Storm Claw), which they use when you meet them at
  level 70.
- Professor Mo and the starter rivals.
