# The plot (draft for you to edit)

Emerald Remix keeps **Emerald's story beats**: every scene, map and event script already exists, so
changing who's in them and what they say is cheap, while new events are expensive. What changes is
*why* it all happens: the three legendary cats, your friends' starters, and two villain teams with
very New York motives. Everything here is a draft; cross out anything and write your own.

## The idea in one paragraph

Long ago, **Haleeb** spread the clouds and **Ceaser** brought the sun, and their endless argument
over the weather nearly wrecked the land until **Snickers** came down from the storm clouds and ended
it with one swipe. Haleeb fell asleep on a cloud bank under the sea (rain falls wherever she naps),
and Ceaser lay down in a cave and hasn't stood up in a century. Now **Ahmed and Omar**, the two
partners behind **Safety Zone**, have split the company in a feud over money, and each wants a
legendary on his side. Professor Mo just wants a cold drink, and for his own Ahmed and Omar (the
Pokémon) to stop fighting too.

## The villains: Ahmed vs Omar

They used to run Safety Zone together. Now they don't speak.

- **Ahmed is mad** that Omar never does any work, and that Omar **deleted Hamza's help review**.
- **Omar is mad** that Ahmed **changed his password** and locked him out of the company.
- Both want the money. Ahmed kept the company; Omar walked out with half the guards and a beanie.

## The heart of the story: keeping the peace

**You and Professor Mo are the peacekeepers**, and you're not very good at it. Mo has had the same
problem at home all along: his Ahmed and his Omar (the Pokémon) fight exactly like their trainers. All
game, the two of you try to get Ahmed and Omar to talk, and it keeps going wrong: one of them storms
off, someone brings up the password, someone brings up Hamza's review. Mo's plan is always food:
"Nobody can stay mad over crazy fries with fluffy sauce." It never works until the very end.

## The ending

After Snickers ends the storm, Ahmed and Omar return the orbs to Mt. Pyre together, and talk it out with
you and Mo, for hours. **Ahmed changes the password back. Omar gives Safety Zone a five-star review on
Yelp** ("Great security. Boss is kind of strict."), and Hamza's review goes back up.

## What's already in the game

- The three cats, with their weather abilities (Haleeb's rain, Ceaser's sun, Snickers calming both),
  so beats 13–17 already play out with them; only the dialogue is still Emerald's beyond the names.
- Their signature moves (Milk Shower, Royal Pounce, Storm Claw), which they use when you meet them at
  level 70.
- Professor Mo and the starter rivals.
- **Ahmed and Omar as the bosses**: names in every line of dialogue, the team names (SAFETY ZONE,
  TEAM BEANIE), the trainer classes (SAFETY BOSS, BEANIE BOSS) and their new teams. Hamza is Ahmed's
  admin. They still wear Maxie's and Archie's sprites, and the dialogue still talks about the land and
  the sea until the scenes are rewritten.
