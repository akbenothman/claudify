# The Starters

Three starter lines with a New York theme. Each one is based on a real city animal.

| | Heesh line | Omar line | Ahmed line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Animal | Sewer rat | City raccoon | Bulldog (guard dog) |
| Theme | Chef, cheese, strawberries, Knicks fan | Beanie, NYC tourist, thief, grimy | Security guard → CEO of **Safety Zone** |
| Stage 1 (Lv 5) | **Squeesh**: Poison | **Beanito**: Dark | **Guardpup**: Fighting |
| Stage 2 (Lv 16) | **Heesh**: Poison/Fire | **Omar**: Dark | **Ahmed**: Fighting |
| Stage 3 (Lv 36) | **Heeshef**: Poison/Fire | **Omarauder**: Dark/Ground | **Ahmexec**: Fighting/Steel |
| Style | Fast special attacker | Fast physical attacker | Slow, bulky physical tank |
| Signature move | Toxic Fondue | Sticky Fingers | Lockdown |

Each line's middle stage carries the friend's name. The first and last stages each get their own kind of wordplay:

- **Squeesh** (squeak + Heesh) → **Heesh** → **Heeshef** (Heesh + chef)
- **Beanito** (beanie + "-ito", little) → **Omar** → **Omarauder** (Omar + marauder)
- **Guardpup** (guard + pup) → **Ahmed** → **Ahmexec** (Ahmed + exec)

All names fit the game's 10-character limit, and any of them can be changed later.

## How the three types beat each other

Each final form gains a second type, which turns the three into a loose rock-paper-scissors (like Grass/Fire/Water):

- **Heeshef beats Ahmexec**: Fire is super-effective on Steel, and Poison/Fire resists both Fighting and Steel.
- **Ahmexec beats Omarauder**: Fighting is super-effective on Dark.
- **Omarauder beats Heeshef**: Ground is 4× super-effective on Poison/Fire.

Omarauder's Ground moves also hit Ahmexec for super-effective damage, so that matchup is closer. Ahmexec has the bulk to win it.

## Heesh line: the Sewer Chef Pokémon

**Concept:** A sewer rat who loves food. He scurries through the tunnels in an orange-and-blue basketball jersey
(a Knicks fan), snacking on cheese and strawberries. When he evolves, he finds a chef's hat and starts *cooking*.
That's where his Fire type comes from: he lights sewer gas to cook.

### Squeesh (Stage 1): Poison
- **Looks:** A tiny grey-brown rat with big round ears, a pink nose, a long pink tail, and big shiny eyes. He wears an
  **oversized orange-and-blue basketball jersey that hangs on him like a dress**. He hugs a strawberry nearly as big as his head.
  There are cheese crumbs on his cheeks.
- **Dex:** *It lives in city sewers and comes out at night to raid kitchens. It will trade anything it owns for a ripe strawberry.*

### Heesh (Stage 2): Poison/Fire
- **Looks:** He's bigger now and stands up on two legs. The jersey fits him, and it has a number on it. He wears a **white chef's
  hat, slightly crooked**. He carries a wedge of cheese, and a strawberry is tucked behind one ear. A small green-purple flame
  flickers on the end of his tail, which works like a stove burner.
- **Dex:** *It cooks with the sewer gas that collects in its tail. Its cheese fondue is said to be delicious but mildly toxic.*

### Heeshef (Stage 3): Poison/Fire
- **Looks:** The **head chef of the sewers**. He's a tall, confident rat with a huge chef's hat, the basketball jersey worn over a chef's
  apron, and a ladle held like a scepter. A whole **wheel of cheese** sits under his arm, and his tail-flame is a big purple fire.
  His expression is smug.
- **Dex:** *It rules a hidden kitchen beneath the city. The toxic stew it cooks can melt steel, but it always saves the strawberries for dessert.*

| | HP | Atk | Def | SpA | SpD | Spe | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Squeesh | 40 | 45 | 40 | 60 | 45 | 80 | 310 |
| Heesh | 55 | 60 | 55 | 80 | 60 | 95 | 405 |
| Heeshef | 75 | 75 | 65 | 115 | 85 | 115 | 530 |

- **Abilities:** Gluttony (eats its held berry early). Hidden ability: Ripen (doubles berry effects), because he loves fruit.
- **Signature move: Toxic Fondue**: Poison, Special, 80 power, 100% accuracy, 30% chance to **burn**.
  This reuses the game's existing "burn chance" effect, so it's cheap to add.
- **Learns along the way:** Poison Gas, Bite, Ember, Sludge, Flame Wheel, Sludge Bomb, Flamethrower, Belch, Toxic Fondue.

## Omar line: the Beanie Pokémon

**Concept:** A grimy city raccoon whose **black beanie is the most important thing about him**. It's the first thing
you see at every stage, and it grows as he evolves. He hides inside it, sleeps in it, and stuffs everything he steals
into it. He loves trips to New York and "collects souvenirs" (he steals them). He never takes a bath, and the mud he's
covered in becomes his Ground type at the final stage.

**Beanie rules for the art:** The beanie is always **solid black with a ribbed, folded-up cuff**. The ribs are drawn
in dark grey so the hat doesn't turn into one black blob at 64×64. His fur is light grey, so the black beanie stands
out against it. At every stage the beanie takes up more of the sprite than his face does.

### Beanito (Stage 1): Dark
- **Looks:** A tiny raccoon kit who **lives inside a black beanie that's far too big for him**. The beanie covers almost all of
  his body like a sleeping bag or a shell. Only his masked face peeks out from under the cuff, his little paws grip the edge,
  and his ringed tail sticks out of the back. The beanie is dusty, and a stolen soft pretzel pokes out of it.
- **Dex:** *It hides inside a black beanie it stole as a baby, and runs off when anyone tries to take it away. The beanie has never once been washed.*

### Omar (Stage 2): Dark
- **Looks:** A raccoon standing on two legs under a **huge, slouchy black beanie** that's nearly as tall as his body. It flops over
  to one side and is so stuffed with stolen souvenirs that they stick out of it: a mini Statue of Liberty, a foam finger, a hot dog.
  He wears an open hoodie with a mud stain, and one paw is reaching up into the beanie to hide something. Under the cuff,
  only his bandit mask and a sly grin are visible.
- **Dex:** *It loves visiting big cities and never leaves without a souvenir. Anything it steals vanishes into its beanie, which seems to have no bottom.*

### Omarauder (Stage 3): Dark/Ground
- **Looks:** A big, burly raccoon, now the **king of the city's thieves**. His black beanie has grown into an **enormous, patched hood
  that drapes down his back like a cape** and drags on the ground behind him. It bulges with stolen loot, and gold and gadgets spill
  out of the holes. Glowing yellow eyes and a wide toothy grin shine out from the shadow of the cuff. Hardened mud covers his arms
  like armor.
- **Dex:** *Its giant beanie holds a whole city's worth of stolen goods. It is so filthy that dirt has hardened into armor on its body.*

| | HP | Atk | Def | SpA | SpD | Spe | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Beanito | 45 | 60 | 45 | 35 | 45 | 80 | 310 |
| Omar | 60 | 80 | 60 | 50 | 55 | 100 | 405 |
| Omarauder | 80 | 115 | 75 | 65 | 70 | 125 | 530 |

- **Abilities:** Pickpocket (steals the item of anyone who hits him). Hidden ability: Stench (he's dirty).
- **Signature move: Sticky Fingers**: Dark, Physical, 75 power, 100% accuracy, **steals the target's held item**.
  This reuses the effect of the move Thief.
- **Learns along the way:** Scratch, Sand Attack, Thief, Mud-Slap, Bite, Knock Off, Bulldoze, Sucker Punch, Crunch, Earthquake, Sticky Fingers.

## Ahmed line: the Security Pokémon

**Concept:** A bulldog who starts out as a rookie security guard, works his way up to head of security, and ends
up as the **boss of Safety Zone**, a security company, in a black suit and tie. His Steel type shows up at the
final stage: the steel briefcase and the corporate armor.

### Guardpup (Stage 1): Fighting
- **Looks:** A short, stocky bulldog puppy in an **oversized security guard uniform**: a navy cap that's too big for him,
  a navy shirt with a yellow "SECURITY" patch, a little flashlight, and a walkie-talkie clipped to his belt. He has a serious face
  and droopy jowls, and he's trying very hard to look tough.
- **Dex:** *It takes its job very seriously and guards whatever it is told to, even a single rock. It never sleeps on duty. Probably.*

### Ahmed (Stage 2): Fighting
- **Looks:** A muscular bulldog standing tall in a **head-of-security outfit**: black tactical vest with a gold badge, an earpiece,
  dark sunglasses, and arms crossed. A spiked collar peeks out under the vest.
- **Dex:** *It has never let an intruder through. Even powerful Pokémon turn back when it lowers its sunglasses and stares.*

### Ahmexec (Stage 3): Fighting/Steel
- **Looks:** A big, broad bulldog who now runs the company. He wears a **sharp black suit with a black tie**, a gold
  **"Safety Zone" shield pin** on his lapel, steel cufflinks, and **steel-plated knuckles**. He carries a steel briefcase that
  doubles as a shield. He stands calm and powerful, and his sunglasses are pushed up on his head.
- **Dex:** *It is the founder and boss of Safety Zone. Its steel briefcase can stop a Hyper Beam, and its handshake can bend iron.*

| | HP | Atk | Def | SpA | SpD | Spe | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Guardpup | 55 | 65 | 60 | 30 | 55 | 45 | 310 |
| Ahmed | 70 | 90 | 80 | 40 | 65 | 60 | 405 |
| Ahmexec | 90 | 120 | 110 | 55 | 85 | 70 | 530 |

- **Abilities:** Guardpup has Inner Focus (a rookie who stays on task) and gains **Intimidate** when it evolves
  into Ahmed, head of security. Hidden ability: the other one. (Intimidate on a level-5 starter made the first
  rival battle unwinnable for Beanito.)
- **Signature move: Lockdown**: Steel, Physical, 80 power, 100% accuracy, **the target can't switch out or flee**.
  This reuses the effect of the move Anchor Shot.
- **Learns along the way:** Tackle, Leer, Rock Smash, Protect, Bite, Brick Break, Bulk Up, Iron Head, Close Combat, Bullet Punch, Lockdown.

## In the game

- **Birch's bag:** Squeesh (left), Beanito (middle), Guardpup (right), replacing Treecko, Torchic and Mudkip.
- **Rival:** May/Brendan always picks the starter that beats yours, and evolves it on the same schedule as in
  the original (Lv 5 and 15 unevolved, Lv 20–34 middle stage):

  | You pick | Rival's line |
  |---|---|
  | Squeesh | Beanito → Omar |
  | Beanito | Guardpup → Ahmed |
  | Guardpup | Squeesh → Heesh |

- **Pokédex:** Hoenn #001–009 (taking Treecko–Swampert's numbers), and at the end of the National Dex.
- **Signature moves** are learned at level 26. Each line's first attacking move of its own type comes at
  level 7 or later, so the rival's level-5 starter can't hit you super-effectively.
- **Petalburg Pokémon Center:** the NPC who explains your starter's strengths and weaknesses describes
  the new types.
- **Tests:** `make check TESTS="Remix"` checks the signature moves, the type triangle, evolution levels and
  Pokédex numbers.

## Game hooks (story ideas, optional)

- **Safety Zone** can be a real company in Hoenn. Ahmexec's portrait hangs in their office, and a Safety Zone guard
  blocks a road until you beat him.
- Heesh fans hang out in the sewer under Slateport; a chef NPC there gives you strawberries.
- An Omar-style thief NPC steals an item from you early on, and you chase him down later.
